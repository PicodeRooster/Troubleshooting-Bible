# The Troubleshooting Bible
### A portable, offline-first reference tool for IT professionals and support teams.

The Troubleshooting Bible is a self-contained knowledge base covering all branches of information technology. Whether it's diagnosing a failing system in a server room with no internet, supporting a user on-site, or routine onboards, this guide is designed with one key idea: **portability.**  Whether that's on a USB drive, a phone, or in a friendly browser view, it is a ready-to-go pocket guide with the intent of being a technician's lifesaver.

Every entry is written with one goal in mind — get you from problem to resolution faster, with less guesswork and fewer context switches.
---
## What's Inside
The Bible is organized into three entry types:

 - Dictionary — Short, plain-English definitions for technical jargon and important vocabulary. Designed for quick look-ups to concepts. 
 - Encyclopedia Entries — Deep-dive reference articles on specific technologies, threat types, system behaviors, and diagnostic concepts. 
 - How-To Guides — Step-by-step procedures for real-world tasks: configuration, remediation, triage, and more. 

## How to Use It
The Bible is designed to portable and fully offline compatible. Save it on a USB drive, an SD card, a phone, or a local folder. Take it with you. Share it freely. It works wherever a browser works.

To open the Bible, launch the index.html file in any modern browser. Use the built-in search to find entries by keyword. From the Home Page, the search will index all content matching your keyword. From outside Home, your search results will be limited to the page you are currently viewing. For example, if you're in the Encyclopedia page, you will only see the top results indexed in the Encyclopedia.  

Depending on your input, you may see the  "Did you mean to search in [Page]?" feature. This was added to facilitate your search results by linking you to content you may be looking for, but is located in outside section of the Bible. 

## A Living Document
This is not a finished product — it's a growing reference. Entries are added as new problems are encountered, new procedures are standardized, and new knowledge is worth preserving. If you solve something the hard way and figure out the right way, it belongs here.
